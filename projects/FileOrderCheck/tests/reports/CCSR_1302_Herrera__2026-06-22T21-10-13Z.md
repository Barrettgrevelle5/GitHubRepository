## Divergence detail (mismatches only)
All 7 mismatches are APP_FALSE_POSITIVE keyword phantoms; root cause is `matchDoc` keyword
lists matching text that appears *inside another form's body or an enumeration list*, not a
title block:

- **p4 social_security (weak):** RIS page. The bare/short "ssi" token matched the
  Section-8/utility-allowance schedule text. Low harm (weak), but a phantom.
- **p20 student_financial + mortgage (both weak):** the Supplemental Rental Application's
  income grid lists "Student Financial Assistance" and its asset grid lists "Mortgage Note
  Held". These are field labels on the supplemental form, not separate documents.
- **p24 pension_verif (STRONG) + rental_worksheet (STRONG) + self_employ_affidavit (weak):**
  the Zero Income Certification (p24) enumerates every income source it certifies the member
  does NOT have — "pensions" (1.e), "Rental income" (1.c), "self-employed resources" (1.i).
  Two of these strong-match. This is the precise "Zero Income Certification that enumerates
  income types" trap called out in CLAUDE.md; it is still firing. **Highest-priority fix.**
- **p45 live_in_aide_affidavit (weak):** Tenant Selection Plan p3 prose ("Live-in aides are
  subject to the same criminal criteria as the Applicant"). The TSP is multi-page (p43-49)
  and its body text keeps generating cross-reference hits.

Suggested (not applied) tightening — same pattern as prior FP fixes: gate these ids on a
title-zone/`allOf` structural signature rather than a single body token; and consider
treating pages already identified as the start of a long prose doc (TSP, Supplemental) the
way `isChecklistPage` skips doc-listing pages, so their bodies stop seeding phantoms. No
edits made (oracle does not modify app code).
